# Modifications & Tuning

This section covers common performance modifications that can change how a car produces power and how it handles increased performance. The focus is on understanding what different upgrades do and how they work with the rest of the vehicle.

## Topics

- [[modifications-tuning/Engine Modifications|Engine Modifications]]
    
- [[modifications-tuning/Brake Upgrades|Brake Upgrades]]
    

Performance modifications should be considered as part of the entire vehicle. Changes to engine power can affect the transmission, tires, brakes, and other systems, while brake upgrades can help the vehicle handle increased performance.
