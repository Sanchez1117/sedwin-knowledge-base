# Ferrari

Ferrari is an Italian manufacturer known for high performance sports cars and racing. The company has a strong connection to motorsports, especially Formula 1, and many of its road cars use technology and ideas that have been developed through racing.

## History

Ferrari was founded by Enzo Ferrari in 1939, and the company produced its first road car in the late 1940s. Racing has remained an important part of Ferrari's identity throughout its history. The company has competed in many forms of motorsport and has used racing experience to develop its road cars.

## Performance Cars

Ferrari produces different types of performance cars, from sports cars to high performance grand touring models. Its lineup has included both naturally aspirated and turbocharged engines, depending on the model and generation.

The Ferrari 812 Superfast is one example of a high performance Ferrari. It uses a naturally aspirated V12 engine and is designed around strong acceleration, high speed, and a more traditional front engine sports car layout.

## Engineering and Design

Ferrari pays close attention to engine performance, aerodynamics, weight, and handling. The design of its cars is not only about appearance. Many exterior features are shaped to help manage airflow and improve the vehicle's performance.

Ferrari also develops different systems to help the driver use the car's performance. Electronics, braking systems, suspension, and drivetrain controls can all work together to improve how the car responds.

Ferrari's combination of racing experience, engine development, and vehicle design has made it one of the most recognizable names in the performance car industry.
