# Porsche 911 GT3 (991.2)

The Porsche 911 GT3 (991.2) is a high performance sports car that combines the familiar shape of the 911 with technology and engineering designed for serious driving. Introduced for the 2018 model year, this generation of the GT3 is especially known for its naturally aspirated engine, high revving character, and sharp handling. Unlike many modern performance cars that use turbocharged engines, the GT3 uses a 4.0 liter flat six engine that rewards drivers who enjoy building engine speed and reaching high rpm. The 991.2 GT3 was offered with both a seven speed PDK transmission and a six speed manual transmission, giving drivers different ways to experience the car. Its combination of power, balance, and driver focused engineering has made it an important model in the history of the 911 GT3.

## Engine and Performance

The 991.2 GT3 is powered by a 4.0 liter naturally aspirated flat six engine that produces 500 horsepower. One of the most interesting parts of this engine is how high it can rev, reaching up to 9,000 rpm. Instead of relying on turbochargers for extra power, the engine builds its performance through high rpm and quick throttle response. The car can be paired with a seven speed PDK transmission or a six speed manual transmission. The PDK is known for making very quick gear changes, while the manual gives the driver more control over the driving experience.

### Driving and Handling

The handling of the 991.2 GT3 is one of the main reasons the car is so respected by performance car enthusiasts. Its rear engine layout gives the 911 a different balance from many other sports cars, but Porsche uses careful suspension tuning and rear axle steering to make the car feel stable and responsive. The GT3 can change direction quickly while still giving the driver a good sense of what the tires are doing.

Compared with the [[McLaren 720S]], the GT3 has a more traditional approach to performance because it focuses heavily on mechanical grip and driver feedback. This makes the 991.2 GT3 appealing to drivers who want a sports car that feels involved and rewarding rather than simply being fast in a straight line.

## Key Features

- 4.0 liter naturally aspirated flat six engine
    
- 500 horsepower
    
- 9,000 rpm redline
    
- Rear wheel drive
