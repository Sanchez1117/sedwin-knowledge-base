# Manual vs. Automatic Transmissions

The transmission is responsible for sending power from the engine to the wheels while helping the engine operate through different speeds. Performance cars can use several types of transmissions, but manual and automatic transmissions are two of the most familiar. Each one gives the driver a different experience.

## Manual Transmissions

A manual transmission requires the driver to select the gears and use a clutch pedal when changing gears. Some drivers enjoy this because it gives them more direct control over when the engine changes gears. Manual transmissions can also make the driving experience feel more involved.

However, a manual transmission requires more work from the driver, especially in traffic. The driver has to coordinate the clutch, gear selection, and throttle while the car is moving.

## Automatic Transmissions

An automatic transmission changes gears without the driver needing to operate a clutch pedal. Modern automatic transmissions can shift very quickly and are commonly used in high performance cars.

Some performance cars use dual clutch transmissions, which can change gears extremely quickly. This can help maintain acceleration while reducing the amount of time between gears. Automatic transmissions can also make a performance car easier to drive in everyday situations.

## Performance and Driving Experience

The choice between a manual and automatic transmission is not simply about which one is faster. Transmission design, gear ratios, software, and the engine all affect performance. A manual transmission can give the driver more involvement, while an automatic or dual clutch transmission can provide fast and consistent gear changes.

Both types can work well in performance cars. The choice depends on the design of the vehicle and the type of driving experience the manufacturer wants to provide.
